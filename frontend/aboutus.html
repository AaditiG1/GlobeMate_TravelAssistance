GlobeMate

GlobeMate is an AI-based travel planning application meant to revolutionize the way travel planning and experience are done. The word "GlobeMate" is a combination of Globe (global travelling) and Mate (a close friend).

Our Story

GlobeMate started as a Final Year Project at London Metropolitan University, aiming to solve the overwhelming problem of travel planning. Today, we help thousands of travelers discover their perfect journeys.
